0-255.pbf is the Open Sans Semibold glyph range used by MapLibre demo tiles:
https://demotiles.maplibre.org/font/Open%20Sans%20Semibold/0-255.pbf

Bundled so Latin/ASCII MGRS and field labels render without a network connection.
Additional Unicode ranges use the online font endpoint and normal browse cache.
